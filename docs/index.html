02 — The business story

A specific issue, not dashboard decoration.

Midway through the reporting period, Consumer Electronics inventory fell beneath reorder targets. The disruption appeared across the network, with Pacific stores most exposed. The important question was not merely “what is the stockout rate?”—it was where to prioritize replenishment before sales and service levels deteriorated.

FindingConsumer Electronics is the highest-risk category during the replenishment disruption, led by Pacific stores.
Recommended actionPrioritize replenishment and supplier-expedite decisions for affected Pacific store/category combinations; monitor fill-rate recovery daily.

03 — Delivery approach

Built to be useful, explainable, and maintainable.

Semantic layer first

Business-friendly names and governed definitions make search productive for non-technical partners.

Metrics validated in SQL

Margin, fill rate, and on-time shipment use weighted formulas to avoid misleading averages.

Audience-specific design

Separate executive, operations, and regional-manager views keep the decision path clear.

Repeatable delivery

Synthetic data generation, documented KPI logic, and TML export make the build portable and reviewable.
